# tela 1.2.0

versionCode **6**, for **Android 10+**. Every edition updates in place over
1.1.0. Tested on the physical tablet (Full), including moving a 65 GB,
7,298-image private library to the SD card.

## A new look

- Floating capsules replace the app bars on every screen, with one bottom
  navigation pill at every width; the wallpaper keeps scrolling behind it.
- Sheets, dialogs, cards and menus share one raised-surface family, and
  menus open below their capsule instead of over its edge.
- The gradient band fades into each screen, Detail's controls float over the
  photo, and the light theme and Monet palettes are fixed where tones were
  missing.

## Sign in to Wallhaven

- Settings can sign in to Wallhaven and fetch your API key for you. It shows
  a loading state, explains a failed page with a Retry button, and says so
  when your account has no API key yet. Signing out clears the site's
  cookies, cache and storage.

## Moving to the SD card

- **Much faster, and it no longer slows down as the card fills.** Each file
  used to list whole folders on the card several times; a 65 GB move had
  slowed to almost 6 seconds per picture. It now holds at about 0.3 s.
- The move screen shows a real progress report: percentage, time left, files
  and data copied, speed, and time elapsed. The notification shows the time
  left.
- Pictures are moved oldest first, so the private library keeps its order on
  the card, and each picture keeps its thumbnail.
- The download pin follows the move, and GPS-tagged photos pass the copy
  check.

## Private library

- Thumbnails are generated much faster, are kept between visits, and appear
  as they load; opening a picture no longer waits behind them.
- Switching tags shows only that tag's pictures.
- New **Newest first / Oldest first** order, remembered between visits. It
  is the order you saved them in.

## Downloads and settings

- Safe and explicit downloads can each have their own folder.
- Settings can find broken images and repair them.
- A batch containing an explicit image lands together under the explicit
  rules.
- A saved browse default keeps its purity; shape keywords and exact ratios no
  longer conflict.

## Installing

Open the downloaded APK and confirm **Update**. Never uninstall to resolve an
update error: it deletes private images, the database, and keys.
